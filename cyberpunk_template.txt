description_full<<EOF_DESC
Cyberpunk 2077: Ultimate Edition is an open-world action-adventure RPG set in Night City, a sprawling futuristic megalopolis driven by power, glamour and body modification. You play as V, a customizable cyber-enhanced mercenary whose choices, relationships, skills and combat style shape a deeply branching journey through the dark future.

The Nintendo Switch 2 Ultimate Edition includes the full Cyberpunk 2077 base game and the Phantom Liberty spy-thriller expansion set in Dogtown. It also benefits from the major systems, gameplay and quality-of-life improvements released for the modern version of the game.

This edition was specifically optimized for Nintendo Switch 2 and supports Joy-Con 2 motion controls, adjustable gyroscope aiming, Joy-Con 2 mouse controls and touch-screen interaction for maps, menus, inventory and hacking. Cross-progression lets supported saves continue between platforms. Two graphics modes are available: Quality mode targets 1080p at 30 FPS, while Performance mode targets up to 40 FPS at 1080p in TV mode and 720p in handheld mode. Handheld play also uses VRR for smoother presentation.
EOF_DESC
